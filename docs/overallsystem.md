# Weak Signal Intelligence -- Detailed Overall System Plan

## Phase A -- Internal Intelligence Foundation

### A1. Historical Internal Data Understanding

-   Analyze sales trends, stock movement, supplier delay, and booking
    demand.
-   Detect early instability before visible crisis.

### A2. Internal Weak Signal Detection

-   Continuous stock drop
-   Rising supplier delay
-   Sudden demand increase

**Output:** Internal weak signals with severity and timestamps.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase B -- Context‑Driven External Sensing

### B1. Internal‑to‑External Mapping

-   Determine which external factors matter based on internal patterns.
-   Enables targeted sensing instead of blind monitoring.

### B2. External Data Acquisition

-   Collect activated signals such as price trends, policy/news,
    currency, and supply hints.
-   Store as processed external indicators.

### B3. External Weak Signal Detection

-   Apply trend, momentum, and keyword‑frequency rules.
-   Produce confidence‑scored external weak signals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase C -- Signal Intelligence Formation

### C1. Weak Signal Filtering & Scoring

-   Remove noise via thresholds and weighted importance.
-   Generate overall risk level (Low / Medium / High).

### C2. Causal Correlation Engine

-   Link internal and external signals into cause‑effect reasoning.
-   Identify forming future risks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase D -- Future Business Understanding

### D1. Impact Prediction

-   Estimate procurement cost rise, margin drop, and time‑to‑impact.

### D2. Decision Recommendation

-   Suggest inventory increase, supplier locking, pricing adjustment, or
    spending delay.

### D3. Story‑Based Alert

-   Convert analytics into clear human‑readable narrative insight.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase E -- Autonomous Intelligence Loop

### E1. Continuous Monitoring

-   Daily internal/external updates and risk recomputation.

### E2. Event‑Based Alerting

-   Trigger alerts only on meaningful change.

**Result:** Self‑operating early warning intelligence system.
