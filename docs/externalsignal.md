# Weak Signal Intelligence -- Brainstormed External Signals Strategy

## 1. Universal External Signal Dimensions

### Economic

-   Inflation, commodity price, currency, interest rate.

### Policy & Regulation

-   Tax discussions, bans, approvals, compliance rules.

### Consumer Behavior

-   Search trends, sentiment shifts, enquiry rise.

### Supply Chain

-   Raw material shortage, logistics delay, vendor instability.

### Technology Disruption

-   Automation adoption, AI innovation, competitor tech shift.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Context‑Driven External Activation

-   External sensing depends on internal business condition.
-   Rising demand → watch price, supply, logistics.
-   Falling sales → watch sentiment, economy, competitors.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Nature of True Weak Signals

-   Slow repetition instead of sudden spikes.
-   Subtle discussions before official news.
-   Behavioral shifts before measurable demand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Multi‑Industry Scalability Logic

-   Same signal has different impact per industry.
-   Requires industry‑specific impact weighting.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Advanced Future Directions

-   Emotional economy indicators (fear, uncertainty).
-   Climate and rainfall influence on demand.
-   Festival and social rhythm effects.
-   Hidden digital behavior like search spikes.

**Goal:** Sense tiny future disturbances before real‑world change
occurs.
